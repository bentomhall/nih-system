# Nerd Origin (5e)
For everyone who says that X class should be a subclass of Y other class (rangers as fighter subclass, barbarians as fighter subclass, sorcerers as wizard subclass, etc)...here's my answer. This is how wizards fit as a sorcerer subclass.

You're not like other sorcerers, who got their casting by being born with it. You had to work for it. Shoving your bespectacled nose into all the books, practicing all day, getting shoved into lockers by the jocks, getting wedgied and called names. And never getting to date the cheerleaders. But you've had enough. You'll show them.

### Genius
You're a genius, not a theater kid. Your spellcasting ability score is Intelligence, not Charimsa.

### Spellbook
When you pick this origin at 1st level, you have cobbled together a spellbook containing the erudite knowledge of the secrets of the universe.

Each time you learn a new spell, you can choose to write an additional one of a level you can cast into your spellbook. These spells do not count as spells known, but when you finish a long rest and have the book at hand you can exchange one of your spells known for a spell in the spellbook, making it known and able to be cast.

In addition, you can cast any Ritual spells in the book without having them known as long as you have the book on hand.

**Replacing the Book.** If you lose your spellbook, you cannot change your known spells until you recreate your spellbook, which costs 10 gp per spell level you had written in there and requires 8 hours of uninterrupted work..

**Scribing Scrolls.** You can scribe scrolls of spells on the sorcerer spell list into your book by paying 10 gp per spell level and taking an hour per spell level. You must have spell slots of the spell's level. This destroys the scroll.

### Spell Efficiency
Starting at 6th level, you have learned to be more efficient than those brute-force jocks. When you finish a short rest, you can regain spell slots with total level equal to half your level, rounded up. In addition you regain half your sorcery points. Once you use this feature, you cannot do so again until you finish a long rest.

### Specialist
Starting at 14th level, choose one of the schools of magic. Adding metamagic to spells from this school costs one fewer sorcery point, down to a minimum of zero. In addition, you gain the effect below matching the school of magic:

- **Abjuration**: When you cast this spell, you gain temporary hit points equal to the spell's level.
- **Conjuration**: Conjured creatures and objects no longer vanish or become hostile when your concentration is broken. In addition you can alter the appearance of conjured creatures and objects.
- **Diviniation**: When you finish a long rest, roll 1d20. You can replace any one d20 roll with the value chosen. When you do so, you have to make a big speech about how you predicted this in advance.
- **Enchantment**: When you are attacked by a creature within 30 feet of you, you can attempt to beguile them as a reaction by spending 1 sorcery point. They must make a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failure, they must target someone else within range or lose the attack.
- **Evocation**: You add your Intelligence modifier to the damage dealt by evocation spells.
- **Illusion**: Illusions you cast that create free-standing phenomena have an area twice as large. All ability checks made to disbelieve your illusions are made at disadvantage.
- **Necromancy**: When you deal damage with a necromancy spell, you regain hit points equal to half the damage dealt. Healing in excess of your maximum hit points becomes temporary hit points.
- **Transmutation**: You learn the polymorph spell; it doesn't count against your spells known. When you cast it on yourself, you retain your normal mental stats.

### Spell Mastery
At 18th level, pick a spell of 2nd level or lower of your favored school (the one you picked for your Specialist feature). You gain the following benefits when you cast this spell:

- You can attach any number of metamagic to it.
- You no longer have to spend a spell slot to cast it at its base level. You still have to pay for any costly metamagic.

## Other changes
The wizard class and spell list vanish from existence. Any wizard-exclusive spells no longer exist as PC-facing options.
