# School of Liminality
L2: Incantations
You learn a common incantation of your choice and can cast it as an incantation without needing the ritual scroll. At 6th level you learn an uncommon incantation and at 10th level a rare incantation.

L2: Emergency Breach
When you would be hit by an attack or fail a saving throw against an ability that causes half damage on a success, you can attempt to shunt some of the force into the Border Shadow as a reaction. Roll 1d8 and add your Intelligence modifier (minimum zero). You reduce damage taken by the amount rolled (to a minimum of zero). This reduction happens before any resistances and immunities are applied.

Alternately, you can expend a spell slot of 1st level or higher when you use this ability. If you do so, roll an additional d8 per level of the slot expended when determining the damage reduction.

*Fracture*: Count the number of 1s rolled on the damage reduction dice and consult the table below.

| Number of 1s | Result |
|:----|:----:|
|1    | Roll any die. On an odd result, nothing happens. On an even result, one other creature chosen randomly within 30 feet of you takes damage equal to the amount reduced      |
|2    | One other creature chosen randomly within 30 feet of you takes damage equal to the amount reduced|
|3    | Two other creatures of the DM's choice within 30 feet of you each take damage equal to the amount reduced|
|4    | One other creature of the DM's choice within 30 feet of you takes damage equal to twice the amount reduced.|
|5+   | All other creatures of the DM's choice within 30 feet of you each take damage equal to the amount reduced|

L6: See the Unseen
You can cast *see invisibility* once without spending a spell slot, regaining the ability to do so when you finish a long or short rest. While it is active, you are also under the effects of *detect magic*, but do not need to maintain concentration.

L6: Walk the Unseen Path
You can cast *blink* once without spending a spell slot, and you always have it prepared and it does not count against your limit of spells prepared. While *blink* is active, you can choose at the end of each of your turns whether you blink out or not.

L10: Everything in its place
When a creature not on its home plane is critically hit by one of your attacks or rolls a 1 on a saving throw against one of your spells, you can force it to make a Constitution saving throw against your save DC. On a failure, it is banished to its home plane as if you had cast *banishment* on it and it had failed the saving throw. This banishment ends if you are incapacitated within a minute after it starts, otherwise the creature must use other methods (such as *plane shift*) to return. Once a creature is banished, you cannot use this ability again until you finish a long rest.

L10: Controlled Fracture
When you cause a *fracture* from your Emergency Breach effect and have 2 or fewer 1s, you can choose which targets take the additional damage. If you have 3 or more 1s, you can choose one target (before the DM makes his choice) to be unaffected by the fracture.

L14 Travel the Planes
You can cast *plane shift* once without expending a spell slot. When you cast *plane shift* (using this free use or not), you do not need a tuning fork and always count as having specified a permanent teleport circle as a destination.
